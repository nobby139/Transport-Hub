Privacy Notice

1. Introduction

This Privacy Notice explains how UK Transport Hub Ltd handles information entered by users. By using this system, you agree to the practices described in this notice.

2. What Information You Enter

UK Transport Hub stores only the information you manually enter into the system, including:

  • Takings and financial entries
  • Drivers logs
  • Pay rate bands
  • Special days and calendar entries
  • Driver preferences and settings

No automatic data collection, tracking, or analytics are performed.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter is used solely to provide the features of UK Transport Hub Ltd, including:

  • Generating weekly and yearly summaries
  • Calculating takings and pay estimates
  • Displaying mileage and duty information
  • Applying your chosen settings and preferences

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally within your UK Transport Hub environment. No information is shared externally, transmitted to third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

5. Security

You are responsible for keeping your login details secure. UK Transport Hub Ltd does not share your account information and does not provide access to your data to any external party.

6. Cookies

UK Transport Hub may use basic session cookies to keep you logged in while using the system. These cookies do not track behaviour, store personal data, or follow you outside the site.

7. Your Rights

You may update or delete any information you have entered at any time using the tools provided within the system. If you stop using UK Transport Hub, you may clear your data manually.

8. Changes to This Notice

UK Transport Hub Ltd may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. Continued use of the system indicates acceptance of any updated terms.

9. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Notice, please contact us at info@transporthub.uk or use the contact options provided within the system.
